The platform

Financial truth is a relationship problem.

Collateral, obligations, facilities, counterparties and policy rules live across different systems. Keel creates a common evidence-aware view without claiming visibility beyond the sources an institution actually connects.

01 / TRACE

What supports what?

Connect assets, claims, facilities and obligations. Identify declared links, potential reuse and conflicting records within source scope.

02 / CALCULATE

What capacity remains?

Apply versioned policy rules to eligibility, encumbrance, haircuts and evidence freshness. Unknown values remain unknown.

03 / PROPAGATE

What changes under stress?

Trace declared shocks through a defined dependency graph and separate modeled effects from observed events.

Trust, evidence & boundaries

Never turn missing data into a clean bill of health.

Every result should preserve the source, observation time, scope, policy version, assumptions and unresolved issues.

EVIDENCE CLASSES

Observed · Derived · Estimated · Simulated · AI-inferred

AI-inferred outputs remain advisory. They do not become canonical facts or capacity inputs without explicit institutional rules and verification.

UNKNOWN IS A STATE

Unverified is not unencumbered.

Missing records, stale evidence, conflicts and unknown encumbrances must produce visible holds or review—not silent assumptions.

PROOF BOUNDARY

Integrity is not truth.

A signature can identify an issuer and protect record integrity. It cannot alone prove legal title, priority, enforceability or an asset's existence.

Keel Assurance Fabric

Financial decisions need a traceable chain of responsibility.

Keel binds the origin of evidence, the systems that shaped an assessment, the policy applied, the human reviewer and the response to changed conditions into one inspectable record.

01 · ORIGINMARK

Evidence origin

Source identity, source type, observation time, ingestion method, evidence class and content fingerprint.

02 · MODELCENSUS

AI and provider register

Model, provider, version, owner, purpose and access scope. AI-inferred material remains explicitly classified and is never promoted to verified evidence.

03 · RULETRACE

Policy reconstruction

Preserves policy identifier, version, thresholds, haircut and input fingerprints used for the assessment.

04 · DECISIONWITNESS

Human accountability

Records reviewer identity, role, acceptance or override status, rationale and the decision fingerprint reviewed.

05 · DRIFTSENTINEL

Change-triggered reassessment

Compares evidence, policy, model, provider and integration fingerprints; material changes raise a reassessment signal.

06 · FALLBACKMANDATE

Bounded recovery

Provider outage, stale evidence, conflict or unknown outcome restricts progression and requires reassessment or human review. No blind financial retry.

Frequently asked

Clear answers for a serious financial product.

Is this connected to live banks or custodians?

No. The live website API and its database are hosted, but the examples are synthetic. Institution-specific data connectors must be designed, authorized and tested separately.

Can Keel prove collateral is unencumbered everywhere?

No. It can only assess records within declared, sufficiently authoritative and current source boundaries. Missing source coverage must remain explicit.

Is the platform ready for production finance decisions?

The site and prototype API are an early platform foundation. Production financial use requires authentication, tenant isolation, security review, authoritative integrations, policy governance, independent testing and institution approval.

How does a plugin designer earn?

Approved partners may negotiate implementation fees, referral fees or recurring revenue share for listed and supported integrations. A free evaluation period can be agreed per partner and customer; no payment conversion or revenue is guaranteed.

Does Keel need blockchain?

No. The initial product uses conventional APIs, database records and cryptographic fingerprints. A distributed ledger is not assumed.
