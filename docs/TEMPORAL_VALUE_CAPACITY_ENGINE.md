# Keel Temporal Value & Capacity Engine (TVCE)

## Product thesis
Convert independently evidenced future productive capacity and contracted service receivables into a controlled present underwriting assessment and service-credit entitlement. Do not create value from a token forecast.

## Separate instruments
1. Capacity Forward: bilateral or platform contract for future delivery of a defined service unit, provider, region, quality, window, price, remedies and substitution rules.
2. Future Utility Credit: prepaid/contract-backed right to consume specified service units. It is not cash, a deposit, general-purpose money or guaranteed redeemable property.
3. Temporal Advance: an institution's separately approved advance against eligible, enforceable receivables or contracted cash flows, with explicit recourse, assignment, reserve, haircut and loss waterfall.
4. Capacity Risk Hedge: future price/availability risk contract. Requires benchmark governance, basis-risk measurement, counterparty margin, liquidity and legal/regulatory analysis.

## Core underwriting rule
The engine calculates a conservative review ceiling from contracted unearned units, contract unit price, verified provider capacity, observed provider performance, buyer payment support, dispute reserves and a versioned policy maximum. The output is not an approval. It is not a token mint, cash balance, legal collateral opinion, future price prediction or promise of repayment. Human institutional underwriting is mandatory.

## Zero-interest distinction
A contract may be structured with no stated interest coupon. That does not mean it has no economic financing cost or no credit risk. Fees, discounting, prepaid price, service delivery margin, recourse, reserves and opportunity cost must be disclosed. Do not claim that compute work automatically repays a loan. Repayment must arise from enforceable buyer payment obligations, assigned receivables, escrowed proceeds or accepted service delivery under a legally reviewed contract.

## Service credit issuance
A credit may only represent an identified right to consume defined service units from a named provider or approved network, subject to expiry, region, hardware class, service level, substitution, refund, insolvency, transfer and redemption rules. The credit balance is capped by reserved/available deliverable capacity and cannot be freely traded or used to settle unrelated corporate debts unless separately legally structured and approved.

## Financial State integration
- Register each forward contract as a commitment with provider, buyer, units, dates, currency, unit price, delivery window and contract fingerprint.
- Register capacity as a distinct resource object, linked to location, hardware, power, network, maintenance and other simultaneous commitments.
- Prevent double sale/pledge of the same GPU-hour or capacity interval.
- Record future utility credits as service obligations, not cash or collateral.
- Record advances and receivables as distinct liabilities/claims; prevent double assignment.
- Reconcile each delivery to metering evidence, workload logs, customer acceptance, disputes and invoice/payment evidence.
- Link every policy, underwriting decision, witness and revision to Authority Chain.

## Financial Fracture integration
Stress provider insolvency, GPU obsolescence, power outage, network failure, chip export restrictions, customer default, benchmark manipulation, compute price collapse/spike, data-centre concentration, correlated demand shock, cloud provider failure, cyber incident and simultaneous failure of multiple providers. Measure undelivered credits, replacement procurement cost, advance recovery shortfall and concentration exposure. Never assume that a compute credit can be redeemed during provider failure.

## Mandatory safety and commercial controls
- No native Keel token or speculative fTOKEN in the first release.
- No algorithmic market maker, AMM, open-market trading or automated FX.
- No lending or credit approval by model alone.
- No live staking of client collateral to GPU providers.
- No collateral rehypothecation or reuse without explicit legal authority and institutional consent.
- No cross-entity or cross-currency offset without legal basis and accounting treatment.
- No issuance above independently verified capacity net of prior reservations and outage reserves.
- Segregate provider capacity, service-credit liabilities, cash proceeds, collateral and financing exposure.
- Independent benchmark sources and basis-risk monitoring before derivatives.
- Human approval, legal review and regulated partner for any actual financing, transferable instrument or settlement.

## Implementation status
The current code is a deterministic simulation/underwriting calculator only. It does not connect to compute providers, validate legal contracts, authenticate the source evidence, create ledger postings, issue credits, execute lending, transfer collateral or settle money. Source attestation and the persistent source registry must be connected before partner data can be marked verified.
